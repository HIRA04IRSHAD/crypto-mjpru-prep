# Video lectures

Every topic of the CS-443T syllabus, in syllabus order, with YouTube lectures that explain it. Watch the short one before reading the unit notes; use the longer one when a topic still feels shaky.

> **How to use this page:** tap a thumbnail to play it here. Neso Academy (English) and Abhishek Sharma, 5 Minutes Engineering and Gate Smashers (Hindi) cover the ciphers and numericals; Computerphile and Udacity's *Applied Cryptography* clips cover the Schneier protocols (ZKP, blind signatures, oblivious transfer) that Indian channels rarely teach. Topics marked ⭐ are the ones MJPRU has asked; see the [PYQ analysis](#analysis).
